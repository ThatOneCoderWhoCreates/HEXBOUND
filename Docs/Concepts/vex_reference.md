# Vex (launcher fighter): look and equip reference

Reference sheet: `vex_reference.png` (provided by Tanmay, 2026-11-15). Models will follow.

## Look
- Dark ponytail; black and gray armored bodysuit with red accents; silver shoulder guards, forearm guards and knee guards.
- Long split coat with silver tribal pattern on the back; belt with pouches; fingerless gloves; heeled boots.

## Weapons
- Missile launcher: large black tube with red panels, top carry handle, rear pistol grip with trigger, front grip.
- Missile: black body, red band, rear and middle fins.
- Pistol: silver slide, black frame, red accent, under-barrel rail.

## How the weapons are carried (bottom-right panels)
- Launcher mode: launcher on the right shoulder, aimed forward; right hand on the rear grip, left hand on the front grip.
- Pistol mode (mouse wheel): launcher slung diagonally across the back on its strap; pistol held in the right hand, raised and aimed one-handed.
- Switching: reach over the shoulder to sling or unsling the launcher while drawing or putting away the pistol.

# Vex rig

`rig_vex.py` turns the decimated Vex model (vex_m.npz: launcher_final.glb with its texture baked into vertex colors by ModlyPrep/glb.py read_textured, reduced to 55k triangles, scaled to 1.75 m) into Resources/Fighters/Launcher.glb with a 17-bone skeleton named for PoseRig. Joint positions are hand-placed for her A-pose; the coat follows the pelvis.

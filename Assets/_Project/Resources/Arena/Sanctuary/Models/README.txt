Modly models for the Celestial Sanctuary map. A .glb here with one of these exact names is used the next time the map loads.

Replace built-in pieces (each scaled to the piece it replaces):
TempleGate.glb     the gate building on the terrace (the portal glow moves into its archway)
CherryTree.glb     every cherry tree
CelestialArch.glb  the stone ring in the sky
Lantern.glb        the stone lanterns (the warm window faces glow)
Banner.glb         the hanging banners on the poles (still sway)
FloatingIsland.glb the far floating islands
ArcaneCrystal.glb  the four crystal pillars (about 2.7 m across, matching their solid area; the shards glow)

Extra scenery:
StonePillar.glb    six pillars at the outer ends of the three bridges
WaterfallCliff.glb four waterfall cliffs rising out of the clouds

The Modly exports were reduced to game-friendly triangle counts (Tools/ModlyPrep). Models should stand upright with their front facing +Z.
